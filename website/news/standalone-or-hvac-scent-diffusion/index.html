Product selection

Standalone or HVAC scent diffusion?

Compare installation arrangements before comparing product names.

06 October 2026 · KWS editorial guide · Concept illustration

Two different installation conversations

A standalone device and a machine intended for HVAC integration have different placement and setup considerations. Start by identifying whether the project needs a device in a room or a proposed connection to an air-handling system. KWS lists several diffuser families; the product detail page is the place to check the information for the exact model.

Bring the building team into the discussion

For an HVAC proposal, involve the person responsible for the ventilation system. Share the system arrangement, operating schedule and proposed equipment location with the supplier. Confirm connection requirements and installation instructions before purchase. A product name containing “HVAC” does not replace a project-specific installation review.

Compare operation as well as purchase price

Refill access, power availability, control settings and maintenance arrangements all affect daily use. Compare these details alongside capacity and dimensions. If the required arrangement is unclear, send a drawing or a short project brief to KWS and ask which models should be considered. Confirm the final equipment and installation scope in writing.

Your inquiry checklist

  • Room placement or HVAC connection
  • System arrangement and airflow information
  • Service access and operating schedule
  • Supplier-confirmed installation scope
