Before a scent project: a simple site survey

Gather the information that makes a supplier conversation more productive.

06 October 2026 · KWS editorial guide · Concept illustration

Make a picture of the space

A useful site brief combines a simple plan with a few photographs. Mark the areas under consideration, entrances, ventilation points, nearby power outlets and possible equipment positions. Add the approximate dimensions and operating hours. The goal is to make the intended environment understandable to the supplier, rather than to create a complex engineering document.

Explain the operating conditions

Describe how the area is used during the day and who will look after the equipment. Note access restrictions, service times and any building requirements. For a proposed HVAC arrangement, involve the building’s ventilation team and ask the supplier what additional system information is needed.

Use the brief to confirm a proposal

Send the information with the product inquiry and request a model discussion. Check the proposed installation, refill arrangement and ongoing service needs. Use a sample or a supplier-approved review process where appropriate. Keep the agreed scope with the quotation so that everyone is working from the same project description.

Your inquiry checklist

  • Plan and photographs
  • Dimensions and operating hours
  • Airflow, access and power information
  • Written proposal and review process
