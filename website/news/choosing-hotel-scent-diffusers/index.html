Fragrance planning

Choosing a scent diffuser for hospitality spaces

Start with the space, the airflow and the way your team will manage the equipment.

06 October 2026 · KWS editorial guide · Concept illustration

Start with a practical brief

A hotel entrance, a reception lounge and a guest corridor serve different purposes. Describe the intended space before choosing a machine. Share the floor area, ceiling height, opening hours and the locations of doors, ventilation outlets and seating. This gives the supplier a useful starting point for a model discussion. A coverage figure in a catalog should be treated as one selection input, rather than a promise that every room will behave identically.

Choose an installation your team can manage

The KWS catalog includes different installation arrangements. Check the exact model for its power supply, refill capacity and mounting requirements. Consider who will refill the equipment, how easily they can reach it, and whether an electrical outlet is available. An accessible service location is often more useful than hiding a machine in a place that is difficult to maintain.

Review a sample in the intended environment

Ask about a sample and a fragrance suitable for the selected machine. Begin with the supplier’s operating instructions and make adjustments through the approved controls. Invite the team responsible for the space to review the result at different times of day. Confirm the quotation, lead time and product-specific documentation before ordering.

Your inquiry checklist

  • Space and installation location
  • Power and refill access
  • Model-specific coverage information
  • Sample, fragrance compatibility and documentation
