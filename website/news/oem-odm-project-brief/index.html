What to include in an OEM / ODM project brief

A clear brief helps turn a first inquiry into a useful product discussion.

06 October 2026 · KWS editorial guide · Concept illustration

Describe the requirement before the customization

Begin with the intended application, product family, target quantity and preferred schedule. Identify any catalog models that are close to the requirement. Explain what needs to change and what can remain standard. This allows the supplier to distinguish a product selection inquiry from a development or customization request.

Make the requested changes specific

Where branding, color, packaging or other changes are needed, provide the relevant artwork or written requirements. Ask the supplier to confirm which options are available for the selected model. Do not assume every product supports every customization. Samples, approval steps and any development costs should be included in the written discussion.

Close the approval loop

Agree which sample or specification will be used as the ordering reference. Confirm product identifiers, quantities, packaging, documentation and commercial terms before production. Keep change requests in a single revision record. The KWS team can discuss OEM / ODM requirements; the confirmed quotation defines the scope for a particular order.

Your inquiry checklist

  • Application, model and quantity
  • Requested changes and artwork
  • Sample and approval process
  • Written specification and quotation

Product selection guidance. Confirm specifications, compatibility, installation and commercial terms for the exact model with KWS.
