A practical guide to touchless soap dispensers

Check refill compatibility, power and installation together.

06 October 2026 · KWS editorial guide

Choose around the washroom routine

A dispenser should suit the people using the facility and the staff maintaining it. Think about the intended mounting position, access for refilling and the expected daily pattern of use. Product capacity is only one part of the decision. The KWS catalog provides model-specific fields for comparing available equipment.

Confirm the refill and power arrangement

Do not assume every dispenser accepts the same liquid or has the same power supply. Confirm the approved refill type, the model’s power requirements and any installation instructions. Ask for a demonstration or sample when the operating behavior is important to the project. Keep the product identifier in your inquiry so that the reply applies to the selected item.

Plan for cleaning and support

Agree who will check refill levels, clean the exterior and follow the operating instructions. Record the model and retain the supplier’s documentation. If a dispenser behaves differently from expected, describe the power arrangement, refill and visible symptoms when contacting support. Avoid opening or modifying a unit unless the supplier’s instructions authorize that procedure.

Your inquiry checklist

  • Exact model and intended location
  • Approved refill type
  • Power supply and mounting instructions
  • Cleaning, refill and support routine
