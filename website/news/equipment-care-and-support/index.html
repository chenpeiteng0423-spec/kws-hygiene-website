Service & support

Building a simple equipment care routine

A clear record helps your team maintain equipment and describe support questions.

06 October 2026 · KWS editorial guide · Concept illustration

Create a model-specific routine

Keep the product instructions available to the people responsible for each device. Record the model, installation location and refill information. Use the supplier’s guidance to define refill checks, exterior cleaning and any routine tasks. A general checklist is useful for organizing work, but it should not replace instructions for the actual product.

Report a problem with useful details

When asking for support, include the product identifier, the circumstances in which the issue appears and any visible indicators. Photographs or a short video can help explain the behavior. Note the power and refill arrangement where relevant. This gives the supplier a clearer basis for advising the next step.

Confirm service terms for the order

Ask about warranty and support arrangements before purchase and keep the confirmed terms with the order records. Installation conditions, applicable documentation and permitted maintenance steps may differ by model. Follow the supplier’s instructions and agree any repair or replacement arrangements before taking action.

Your inquiry checklist

  • Instructions and product identifiers
  • Refill and exterior-cleaning checks
  • A clear description of support symptoms
  • Order-specific warranty and service terms

Product selection guidance. Confirm specifications, compatibility, installation and commercial terms for the exact model with KWS.
