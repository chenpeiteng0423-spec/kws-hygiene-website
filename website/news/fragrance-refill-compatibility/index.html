Fragrance refills: confirm compatibility first

Match the refill to the device and follow the supplied instructions.

06 October 2026 · KWS editorial guide

Treat the machine and fragrance as a combination

The KWS product range includes fragrance equipment and essential oil products. Before ordering a refill, identify the exact diffuser model and the intended fragrance product. Ask the supplier to confirm compatibility and the instructions for that combination. A product category name alone does not establish that every liquid can be used in every machine.

Keep the operating instructions close at hand

Review filling, storage and operating guidance supplied with the selected product. Follow any product-specific labeling and documentation. Do not mix refills or substitute a liquid solely because its appearance is similar. If documentation is missing or unclear, request it before use.

Keep repeat orders easy to identify

Record the fragrance name or code, the machine model and the supplier’s confirmed refill details. Include those identifiers in later inquiries so that a replacement can be checked against the previous order. When considering a new fragrance, discuss a sample and review it in the intended setting before confirming a larger purchase.

Your inquiry checklist

  • Exact diffuser and refill identifiers
  • Supplier-confirmed compatibility
  • Filling and storage instructions
  • A record for repeat orders

Product selection guidance. Confirm specifications, compatibility, installation and commercial terms for the exact model with KWS.
