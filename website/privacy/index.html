How this website handles inquiry drafts and external contact links.

Inquiry drafts

The inquiry form prepares a draft in your browser. It does not submit your details to a website database or send a message automatically. The draft is not stored in local storage by this website.

Email and WhatsApp

Choosing an email or WhatsApp link opens the selected service with your draft. Review it before sending. Your email provider or WhatsApp handles the message under its own privacy terms.

Images, videos and browsing

Product images and migrated videos are served with this website. This version does not include marketing analytics or advertising trackers. The hosting provider may retain ordinary access logs.
