A clear production process

Attention at every stage.

The source factory description identifies a 5S workshop management approach, from material purchasing to manufacture, testing and packing.

01

Define the requirement

Confirm application, product model, quantity and the intended installation. A clear brief helps the team identify the right starting point.

02

Review samples & options

Discuss samples, OEM / ODM requests and packaging options. Agree the approved specification and any artwork before ordering.

03

Materials & manufacture

Material purchasing and manufacturing follow the confirmed product requirements. Confirm any model-specific requirements with the team.

04

Inspect & test

The factory information describes product testing before shipment. Ask which inspection and documentation apply to your selected model.

05

Pack & prepare

Confirm protective packaging, quantities and shipment terms in the quotation. Keep the approved product and packaging references together.

06

Support everyday use

Retain operating instructions and the product identifier. Agree the training, warranty and support arrangements for your order.
