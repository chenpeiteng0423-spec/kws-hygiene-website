KWS Scent Air Fragrance Diffuser Hotel HVAC 500ml Metal Air Commercial Aroma Machine

Description:

Dongguan KWS Hygiene Industrial Co.,Ltd Is A Professional Enterprise Integrating The Production And Marketing Of Scent Air Machine,Aerosol Dispenser,Fan Dispenser,Refillable Liquid Dispenser,Essential Oil,Soap Dispenser,Paper Dispenser,Sanitary Bin And Urinal Mat Etc For More Than 12 Years.

Our equipment has an advanced atomizing system integrating the world-leading cold air diffusing technology, through which essential oil is atomized into nano molecules and diffused into the air, in realization of lasting fragrance in the air. The technology is currently the only diffusing technology in the world that can maintain the integrity of essential oil. Nano particles formed through atomization by using the technology may diffuse faster and stay longer with the optimal natural quality of essential oil retained, so that use of essential oil can be more economical and effective.
