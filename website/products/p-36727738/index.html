Aromatherapy diffusers have a compressor within their system that works on cold air pressure. It converts essential oils into molecules called nanoparticles and disperses them into the air.

Afterwards, the essential oil molecules are dispersed into the air in the form of a dry mist. The main advantage of this method of smelling is that the mist does not come off like a spray, but stays in the air. Essential oil particles permeate the air, ensuring that the air is always filled with fragrance.

The end result is a pure, long-lasting, high-quality scent without any residue.

There are different types of essential oil diffusers, and they vary in the way they diffuse their aroma. They also differ in design and range of functions.

Operation Instruction

1. Confirm the power switch is in the state of OFF, and to load two " AA " Size batteries. Suggest using two alkaline batteries to make it more effective.

2. While switch to TEST function, the Pump Spray dispenser will automatically spray every 5 seconds for test or demo.

3. User can adjust and set up spray interval from 7.5/ 15/ 30minute selection as he likes.

4. Similarly, user can adjust his favorite operation mode from 24H/NITE/DAY.

5. The following diagram will indicate how to load one refill can into the right position of dispenser housing.

6. Turn on the power. The dispenser will activate as before selected operation mode continuously. If found it is not activated yet, user should check INT/OFF switch.

7. Continuously per 5 seconds flashing green LED light indicates that dispenser is in good condition.

8. Red flash indicates the spray time is over 2500 times, and one refill might be used up. Then what you have to do is to check the refill. Replacing one new refill, you should turn on/off the power switch again, the spray record would restart from 0, and the red alarm flash will stop
