Description

Anti Splash Urinal Screen Mat for Man Restroom Toilet
This Urinal Screen Mat 2.0 offers the perfect solution for urinals with enhanced fragrance delivery and splash back protection for up to 30 days or 5000 flushes. 
By drastically reducing splash back, these screens mats also protect the floors from messes, while reducing the spread of germs throughout a facility.
This deodorizer is ideal for bathroom, restroom, office, restaurant , school, health care facilities and industrial location.
Features for Urinal Screen Mat
1. Each urinal screen can release fresh scent which can last up to 30 days or 5000 flushes.
2. Eliminate 99% splash back.
3. Prevent blocking by resisting cigarette, paper, chew gum.
4. Different Fragrances and colors available.
5. Individually wrapped by PE bag, lock fragrance well.
6. EVA material, Eco-friendly and recyclable.
7. Various size and design.
8. Patented certificates for different models and types.
9. Professional R&D team, private label available.
Directions for Urinal Screen Mat
1. Open package and take urinal screen out.
2. Put it on urinal directly
3. When the scent become fade and non, then its time to change a new one.
