The details.

KWS PP Plastic Aromatherapy Diffuser HVAC Scent Air Machine

Description

This equipment uses the world's leading cold-air fragrance diffusion technology to atomize essential oils into nano-molecules into the air through an advanced atomization system, leaving a lasting fragrance in the air. This technology is currently the only diffusion technology in the world that can maintain the integrity of essential oils. The use of this technology to atomize into nanoparticles can diffuse faster in the air, maintain a longer time, and maintain the best natural quality of essential oils to the greatest extent possible. It is also more economical and effective. It is the best choice for hotel lobbies, meeting rooms, multi-function halls, SPA fitness clubs, shopping centers, real estate companies, car showrooms, concert halls, photo studios, art galleries and other indoor places to improve the environment and create atmosphere.
