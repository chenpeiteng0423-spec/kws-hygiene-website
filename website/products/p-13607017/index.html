The details.

KWS ABS Plastic Mini Portable Battery Ultrasonic Car Scent Diffuser Machine

Product Advantages

1. Intelligent time setting, flavor concentration adjustable.
2. Using the principle of two-fluid atomization, cold mist diffusion system.
3. Ultra-quiet atomizing core.
4. Low oil consumption.

Technology

Principle: the machine is to use Venturi nanotechnology diffusing fragrance oil into particles, which spread out as visible mist.
HVAC connection: the machine is connected via an hose attached to a small PU tube which is installed in the A/C duct or you can put the machine on the ceiling near the air outlet or inlet.
