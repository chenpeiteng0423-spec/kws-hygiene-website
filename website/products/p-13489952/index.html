KWS 500ml Aluminum Alloy Aroma Diffuser For Large Area Scent Air Machines

Description

All-in-one business diffuser, automatic timing function, uniform fragrance diffusion, long-lasting fragrance, fine atomization and no residue, ultra-quiet, energy saving and environmental protection, humane design, diffusion area up to 2500m3 .The equipment has an advanced atomizing system integrating the world-leading cold air diffusing technology,throught which essential oil is atomized into nano molecules and diffused into the air,in reslization of lasting fragrance in the air.The technology is currently the only diffusing technology in the world that can maintain the integrity of essential oil.Nano particles formed through atomization be using the technology may diffuse faster and stay longer with the optimal natural quality of essential oil retained,so that use of essential oil can be more economical and effective.
