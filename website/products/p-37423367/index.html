Description of Scent Air Machines:

Scent air machines, also known as Essential Oil diffuser or scenting marketing systems, are devices that release artificial scents or fragrances into the air to create a more pleasant and inviting environment. These machines work by using a combination of technologies, such as essential oil diffusion, nebulization, and ultrasonic vibration to create and distribute fragrance particles into the air.

Advantages of Ceramic Scent Diffuser Machine:

Magnetic air pump is used, with a service life of 4,000-10,000 hours. Good silent effect, less than 30db.
Advantages: Long service life and good stability.

With reference to the principle of the two-fluid atomizing nozzle in combination with the characteristics of the fragrance essential oil, a nano-scale two-fluid atomizing nozzle has been developed. With the nano-scale atomization of the essential oil particles, as the particles are very small, no essential oil is scattered on the ground near the machine, and the fragrance molecules are completely diffused in the air.

Two filtration systems (gas and liquid) are added, and the gaps and the hole diameters (as core parts) are properly made inside the nozzle. Air pumps of different powers match nozzles of different hole diameters to make the nozzles free of blocking.

Cost saving, fuel efficient, good diffusion effect and good fragrance evenness. The nozzles free of blocking to reduce a lot of after-sales services.

resistant to acid, alkali and corrosion, and has good hole diameter stability, and good surface finish quality and no blockage.
