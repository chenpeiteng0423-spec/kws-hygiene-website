7. Wall mounted, easy assemble to a multiple unit.

3 kinds of liquid discharge methods


A. spray: The automatic hand disinfection machine flows out in the form of spray, puts disinfectant, and disinfects the hand or objects.
B. drip: The liquid of the automatic drip soap dispenser flows out in the form of drip, which can be used with ordinary/gel type hand sanitizer and detergent.
C. foam: The liquid of the automatic foam soap dispenser flows out in the form of foam, and a special foam soap liquid needs to be used.
