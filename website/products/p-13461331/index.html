KWS Electric Floor-Standing Scent Essential Oil Diffuser Fragrance Scent Air Machine

Description

All-in-one business diffuser, automatic timing function, uniform fragrance diffusion, long-lasting fragrance, fine atomization and no residue, ultra-quiet, energy saving and environmental protection, humane design, diffusion area up to 2500-3000 m3.The equipment has an advanced atomizing system integrating the world-leading cold air diffusing technology,throught which essential oil is atomized into nano molecules and diffused into the air,in reslization of lasting fragrance in the air.The technology is currently the only diffusing technology in the world that can manitain the integrity of essential oil.Nano particles formed through atomization be using the technology may diffuse faster and stay longer with the optimal natural quality of essentail oil retained,so that ues of essentail oil can be more economical and effective.

Features

1.Two-fluid atomization doesn't damage the molecular structure of essential oil and doesn't affect the diffusion of essential.


2. Three-stage arbitrary regulation. It can be adjusted the working hours and spray interval. It can be controlled accuratelly to seconds, both can achieve the best effect and prevent unnecessary essential oil consumption.


3. Automatic memory function after power off : Avoid the working time to zero after power failure. When changing essential oil, work normally according to the original setting after being powered on.


4.Automatic screen lock: in order to prevent others from touching or changing the correct set parameters of the machine arbitrarily, the product will enter the auto lock screen state 20 seconds after being set.


5.Mute: the noise produced by the product is less than 30dB, which meets the environmental noise standard of urban area and covers a large area.
