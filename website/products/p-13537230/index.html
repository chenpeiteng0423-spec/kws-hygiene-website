Features

1. Two-fluid atomization doesn't damage the molecular structure of essential oil and doesn't affect the diffusion of essential.
2. Three-stage arbitrary regulation. It can be adjusted the working hours and spray interval. It can be controlled accuratelly to seconds, both can achieve the best effect and prevent unnecessary essential oil consumption.
3. Using CNC precision engraving processing technology, Which can be accurate to 0.001mm. The motion, atomized particles more nanometer level fragrance diffusion more uniform ,and can prevent oil leakage and fragrance to be much concentrated situation.
4. Automatic memory function after power off : Avoid the working time to zero after power failure. When changing essential oil, work normally according to the original setting after being powered on.
5. Automatic screen lock: in order to prevent others from touching or changing the correct set parameters of the machine arbitrarily, the product will enter the auto lock screen state 20 seconds after being set.
6. Mute: the noise produced by the product is less than 45dB, which meets the environmental noise standard of urban area and covers a large area.
