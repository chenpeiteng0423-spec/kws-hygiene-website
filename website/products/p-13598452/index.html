Application

Bathroom and shower
Add 5-10 drops to the hot water bath or sprinkle in the shower steam before entering the home spa experience.

massage
8-10 drops of essential oil per 1 ounce of carrier oil. Apply a small amount directly to the relevant area, such as muscles, skin or joints. Gently apply oil to the skin until completely absorbed.

Inhalation
Inhale the aroma steam directly from the bottle, or drop a few drops in the burner or diffuser to fill the scented room.
