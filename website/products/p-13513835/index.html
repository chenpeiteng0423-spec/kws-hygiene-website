KWS Plastic Bathroom Liquid Foam Alcohol Manual Soap Dispenser

Features

1. Spray/Drop/Foam Pumbs are oprtional. Suitable for a variety of viscous liquids, such as: alcohol, gel, hand sanitizer, dishwashing liquid, disinfectant, soap, etc.

2. Molded by durable anti-acid ABS raw material；

3. Ideal for compact washrooms with limited space；

4. Holds up to 400ml with long rubber tube lotion pump；

5. 350ml refillable bottle ,easy to load out and maintain；

6. Recessed clear window for observing liquid soap level；

7. Wall mounted, easy assemble to a multiple unit.
